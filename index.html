Research Interests

My research spans interpretability, AI agents, and embodied intelligence. I study how models represent the steps in a chain of reasoning, how agents check the evidence they retrieve, and how robots apply a familiar skill to an unfamiliar object.

Academic Background

After completing a joint undergraduate program in mechanical engineering at Chongqing University and the University of Cincinnati, I earned my M.Eng. at UC Berkeley, where I worked with Mark Mueller on energy-efficient drone flight. I later held overlapping research assistant positions at Stanford HAI and Peking University.
