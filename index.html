About Me

I am a research assistant at Peking University’s Institute for Artificial Intelligence, where I work with Haoxuan Li. I previously worked with Fei-Fei Li’s group at Stanford HAI and studied robotics at the University of California, Berkeley.

Research Interests

  • Interpretability. I study how models use visual evidence and how their hidden representations change as they reason.
  • AI agents. I study how agents plan, decide which evidence to retrieve, and check whether it supports an answer.
  • Embodied intelligence. I study how robots turn goals into actions and apply familiar skills to new objects.
