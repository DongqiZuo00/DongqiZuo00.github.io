About Me

I studied mechanical engineering through a joint undergraduate program at Chongqing University and the University of Cincinnati. I earned my M.Eng. at UC Berkeley, where I worked with Mark Mueller.

Research Interests

  • Interpretability. When a model answers a question about an image, I want to know which visual details support each step of its answer. I also study what happens inside a language model when it finds two different ways to solve the same problem.
  • AI agents. I study how an agent decides what evidence it still needs and whether the facts it has gathered are enough to answer a question. I’m also interested in how an agent can use its own learning progress to choose what to practice next.
  • Embodied intelligence. I study how robots use vision and language to plan actions and anticipate their effects. One question I explore is how a robot that learned to pour with one container can recognize what to change when it picks up a different one.
