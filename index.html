Research Interests

My research spans interpretability, AI agents, and embodied intelligence. I study how models represent the steps in a chain of reasoning, how agents check the evidence they retrieve, and how robots apply a familiar skill to an unfamiliar object.

Research Experience

  • Interpretability and reasoning. I developed Perception Chains to trace each step of a multimodal answer to the image region and attribute it uses. It reached 72.6% grounding accuracy on DIOR-RSVG, compared with 61.7% for Ferret. In CRF and GRT, I studied how models recover from reasoning errors and preserve multiple correct solutions.
  • AI agents and self-improvement. I co-developed ProvableRAG to check which facts would answer a question and whether the retrieved evidence supports the answer. In RepRSI, I used a model’s internal representations to guide its next round of training. This reduced the error in predicting subsequent accuracy from 19.3 to 10.1 percentage points.
  • Embodied intelligence and robot control. I tested how vision-language-action models transfer skills to new objects in LIBERO. In RepScribe, I used spatial reasoning errors to generate new training examples. In RADI, I used a language model to predict the effects of household actions and revise plans in VirtualHome. My flight-planning work used a recurrent network to predict energy consumption, reducing energy use by 34% relative to RAPPIDS.
