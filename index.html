Dongqi Zuo

My research focuses on interpretability, AI agents, and embodied intelligence. I study how models use visual evidence to reason, how agents decide what information to retrieve, and how robots apply familiar skills to new objects.

I work with Haoxuan Li at Peking University’s Institute for Artificial Intelligence. I previously worked with Fei-Fei Li’s group at Stanford HAI and studied robotics at the University of California, Berkeley.
